🧒 Child Development

Factors Influencing the Learning Process of a Child

The interaction of psychological, physical, family, teacher, and environmental factors
💡 What is learning? Learning is a relatively lasting change in knowledge, skills, attitudes, or behaviour resulting from experience, practice, observation, or instruction. A child's learning depends on the interaction of many factors — no single factor tells the whole story.

🗂️ Major Factors Influencing Learning
🧠
A. Psychological Factors
  • Intelligence: understanding and reasoning.
  • Motivation: encourages effort and achievement.
  • Attention: supports concentration.
  • Memory: helps retain and recall information.
  • Interest & attitude: influence participation.
  • Emotions: confidence supports learning; excessive anxiety may hinder it.
💪
B. Physical & Developmental Factors
  • Age and maturation.
  • Physical health and nutrition.
  • Adequate sleep and rest.
  • Vision, hearing, and motor development.
  • Developmental readiness for learning tasks.
👨‍👩‍👧
C. Family & Social Factors
  • Parental encouragement and guidance.
  • Home environment and study habits.
  • Peer group and friendships.
  • Language and cultural background.
  • Socioeconomic conditions and access to resources.
🏫
D. Teacher & School Factors
  • Teacher's knowledge, behaviour, and attitude.
  • Teaching methods and learning activities.
  • Classroom management and discipline.
  • Curriculum and learning materials.
  • Assessment, feedback, and encouragement.
🌍
E. Environmental Factors
  • Classroom lighting and ventilation.
  • Noise level and seating arrangements.
  • Safety, cleanliness, and emotional security.
  • Availability of books and technology.
  • Opportunities for practice and participation.

Extrinsic Motivation
Learning for rewards or to avoid punishment.
ZPD

Reinforcement
Strengthens the likelihood of a behaviour — B. F. Skinner.
Observational Learning

Law of Effect
Satisfying consequences strengthen responses — Edward Thorndike.
Readiness & Maturation
Associated with Arnold Gesell.
👩‍🏫 Role of the Teacher
  1. Motivate learners and encourage curiosity.
  2. Use age-appropriate teaching methods.
  3. Provide a safe and inclusive classroom.
  4. Consider individual differences.
  5. Give constructive feedback and regular practice.
🔑 Key Takeaway: A child's learning depends on the interaction of psychological, physical, family, social, teacher-related, and environmental factors. Effective teaching recognizes these factors and supports each learner's needs.
